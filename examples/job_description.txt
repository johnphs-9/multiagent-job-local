Senior Data Scientist

We are looking for a data scientist who can translate business questions into
measurable machine-learning solutions. The role partners with product and engineering,
builds and evaluates predictive models, communicates trade-offs to non-technical
stakeholders, and helps improve production ML practices.

Requirements:
- Strong Python, SQL, statistics, and supervised machine learning
- Experience evaluating models and avoiding data leakage
- Clear stakeholder communication
- Familiarity with deployment, monitoring, and experimentation
- Ability to mentor teammates and work across functions
